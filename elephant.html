Synopsis

Commissioned to mark sixty years of diplomatic relations between Korea and Thailand, the film traces the life of Lee Keongson, a Korean film director who settled in Thailand. Through letters exchanged with Lee’s daughter, Muttita Fletcher (Ryeo Lee), it follows what remains of his life in exile and asks how a family’s memories find their place in history.
