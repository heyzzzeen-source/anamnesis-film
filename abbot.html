Synopsis

Before dawn, prayer begins at the Benedictine Holy Spirit Abbey in Mvimwa, Tanzania. Abbot Pambo Martin Mkorwe shares the rhythms of prayer and work with his fellow monks, celebrates Mass in nearby villages and visits local schools. Following his journeys between the abbey and its neighbors, the film explores the place of monastic life within the wider community.
