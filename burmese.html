Synopsis

Soe, Joe and Tommy come from a land they call Burma. They work in Korea without papers. Years of hard labor have worn them out, but they hold on to one source of pride: providing for their families back home. On weekends, they console themselves with bottles of hard liquor.

After a decade, Soe and Joe are determined to finally go home. Tommy won’t go back until he can return in style, with a good sum of money.
