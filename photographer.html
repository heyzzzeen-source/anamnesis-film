Memories of a Photographer

어느 사진가의 기억

A film by Changmin Lee

Starting from an exhibition held after the death of photographer Kim Young-soo, the film follows the pictures he left behind and the people who remember him.

Synopsis

An exhibition opens after the death of photographer Kim Young-soo. Following his photographs and the recollections of those who knew him, the filmmaker retraces Kim’s life amid the avant-garde and cultural movements of the 1970s and 1980s.

As his own connection to Kim emerges, the search becomes an inquiry into what a photograph can preserve and why we make images.
