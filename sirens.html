Synopsis

A filmmaker is writing a screenplay about the end of the world. When disasters resembling those he has imagined begin to appear in the news, he stops writing and withdraws to his study. An unexplained sound lingers in his ears. At first it sounds like radio static. Then it begins to resemble the song of a humpback whale.

Following the sound from the Bangudae petroglyphs in Ulsan to the waters off Okinawa, he encounters traces of how humans have depicted, hunted, and watched whales. Moving between documentary and fiction, the film listens for what the whale’s song might reveal about the stories humans tell when they imagine the end of their world.
