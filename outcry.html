Short documentary · 2017

The Outcry of the People

함성들

A film by Changmin Lee, from the omnibus documentary Candle in the Wave

Outside the National Assembly on the day the impeachment motion passed, the film looks back on the squares of earlier generations and asks what today’s outcry can change.

Synopsis

On December 9, 2016, crowds gather outside South Korea’s National Assembly as lawmakers vote on the president’s impeachment. The film brings the sounds and images of that day into conversation with earlier moments of collective protest in 1960 and 1987. As voices rise again, it asks what a gathering in the square can change.
