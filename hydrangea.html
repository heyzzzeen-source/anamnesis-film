Synopsis

Grandma and her house have aged. With few memories of her, I travel from South Korea to Japan to help her move. What begins as a simple act of help unsettles me.

Born in Japan and raised in South Korea, I learned about Japan through colonial history at school, while friends asked me to translate Japanese lines from adult videos. Why did I come to hide my Japanese name—and one side of myself—as a child? Like a hydrangea changing color with the soil, I begin to see how language, family, and history shaped the color of my memories.
